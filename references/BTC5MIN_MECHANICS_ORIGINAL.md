# Official Polymarket BTC 5m mechanics

Verify live. Config can change per market.

## Identity

- Event series: `btc-up-or-down-5m`
- Market slug: `btc-updown-5m-<unix_start>`
- Window length: 300 seconds, aligned to UTC epoch multiples of 300
- Outcomes: `Up`, `Down`
- Discovery: `GET https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/markets?slug=btc-updown-5m-<ts>`
- Do not sort Gamma by `startDate` and take the first row. That can return a future pre-open market.

## Resolution

Current rule (TWAP era):

> This market will resolve to "Up" if the time-weighted average price (TWAP) of Bitcoin, generated by Chainlink, of the time range specified in the title is greater than or equal to the price at the beginning of that range. Otherwise, it will resolve to "Down".

- Source: Chainlink Data Streams, not Binance/Coinbase/Bybit/OKX last.
- Both open (price to beat) and close come from the same TWAP feed.
- `>=` means equal prints resolve Up.
- Read `raw.cryptoMarketConfig.twapLookbackSeconds` every window.

### Settlement source calendar

| Period | Settlement series |
|---|---|
| Before 2026-08-07 00:00 UTC | Instantaneous Chainlink BTC/USD stream |
| 2026-08-07 to ~2026-08-14 | 5m used 30s TWAP |
| From 2026-08-14 00:00 UTC (markets deployed since Aug 14) | 5m uses 60s TWAP |

TWAP cannot be reconstructed exactly from ~1 Hz instantaneous ticks.

## Data planes

| Plane | Endpoint | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Gamma | `https://gamma-api.polymarket.com` | metadata, slug, tokens, config |
| CLOB REST | `https://clob.polymarket.com` | book, price, tick size |
| CLOB WS | `wss://ws-subscriptions-clob.polymarket.com/ws/market` | book, price_change, last_trade, best_bid_ask |
| RTDS | `wss://ws-live-data.polymarket.com` | Chainlink spot + TWAP |

RTDS TWAP topics: `crypto_prices_twap_thirty`, `crypto_prices_twap_sixty`.
No snapshot or replay after disconnect. Ping every 5s.

Reported collector lag order of magnitude (one public sample day): TWAP p50 ~250ms, p95 ~370ms. Treat as stale if older than config `max_data_age_ms`.

## Fees and constraints

Crypto category taker fee:

`fee = shares * 0.07 * p * (1-p)`

Makers 0. Peak dollar fee at p=0.50 (1.75 cents per share). Docs: https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees

Read per market:

- `orderPriceMinTickSize` / book `tick_size`
- `orderMinSize` / book `min_order_size` (often 5)
- fee schedule fields if present

Tick size can change when price is extreme (`tick_size_change` WS event). Stale tick size causes rejected orders.

## What invalidates a strategy

- Using exchange last price as if it were settlement.
- Backtesting snapshot-era bots on TWAP-era markets.
- Hardcoding 30s lookback after the Aug 14 60s migration.
- Ignoring taker fee at 50c (needs ~1.75 points just to break even before spread).
- Assuming fills at mid.
- Restarting mid-window without stored open TWAP.
- Trading last 5 seconds against a 60s average.
